\section{Error Analysis and Results}

\textcolor{red}{For kink neurons, we wonder whether more accurate propagation-based methods could be developed by parametrizing the distributions more carefully. For instance, multivariate probit models \citep{cragg1971some, chib2001metropolis} might provide a framework for capturing the joint behavior of neurons near the ReLU threshold. For on neurons, we know that they become increasingly heavy-tailed with depth, characterized by sub-Weibull distributions \citep{vladimirova2019understanding}. This suggests that the Gaussian approximation used in our diagonal propagation pass may be systematically underestimating the contribution of on neurons in later layers, even though they are treated as linear. Understanding how to better propagate these heavy-tailed distributions, or how much this approximation error matters in practice, remains an open question.}
